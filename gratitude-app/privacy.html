Gladmoment Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Gladmoment helps you pause during the day and note what you're grateful for. Your gratitude entries can be very personal, so the app is built to keep them on your device.

What the app stores

Your gratitude entries, daily reflections, optional moods, favourites, your optional display name and your reminder and appearance settings. These are saved only in your browser's (or phone's) storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Gladmoment has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your journal is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Voice input

If you choose "Say it", your device's speech recognition turns your words into text. The app does not record or keep audio. Some browsers, such as Google Chrome, send the audio to the browser maker's speech service to transcribe it; their privacy policy applies to that step. You can always type instead.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are used only for your gratitude reminders, never for marketing. You can turn reminders off at any time with the bell.

Your control

You can edit or delete any entry, export everything as a file, delete all your history, or erase the app completely from Settings. Clearing your browser's site data or uninstalling the app also removes your journal, so export a copy first if you want to keep it.

Children

Gladmoment does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@gemrhythm.com
